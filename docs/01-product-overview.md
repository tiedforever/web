# 01 – Product Overview

## Purpose

Tied Forever is an all-in-one wedding planning platform designed to help couples organise every aspect of their wedding from a single, collaborative workspace.

The application replaces the need for multiple spreadsheets, notebooks, messaging apps, email threads and disconnected planning tools by providing one central location for planning, organising and tracking every stage of the wedding journey.

Rather than focusing on a single aspect of wedding planning, Tied Forever brings together task management, guest management, budgeting, supplier tracking, timelines, seating plans, documentation and note-taking into one cohesive application.

The platform is designed to be intuitive enough for couples planning their first wedding while remaining flexible enough to support different wedding styles, sizes and planning preferences.

---

# Product Vision

Planning a wedding should feel exciting rather than overwhelming.

Tied Forever aims to become the single source of truth for everything related to a wedding. Every important piece of information should exist in one place, be easy to find, and be shared between everyone involved in planning.

The long-term vision is to create a platform that grows with a couple throughout the planning process, reducing stress, improving organisation and removing the need to switch between multiple applications.

---

# Problem Statement

Most couples currently plan their wedding using a mixture of:

- Spreadsheets
- Notes applications
- Shared documents
- Email conversations
- Messaging applications
- Physical folders
- Wedding websites

This approach creates several common problems:

- Information is scattered across multiple platforms.
- Tasks become difficult to track.
- Supplier information is spread across emails and notes.
- Budget calculations become inconsistent.
- Important documents are difficult to locate.
- Planning progress is hard to visualise.
- Collaboration between partners becomes fragmented.

As the wedding approaches, managing these disconnected sources becomes increasingly difficult and time consuming.

---

# Solution

Tied Forever provides a single workspace where every aspect of the wedding can be planned and managed.

Instead of storing information in separate applications, each planning area becomes a dedicated module within the platform.

Current planning modules include:

- Dashboard
- Checklist
- Guests
- RSVP Management
- Budget
- Suppliers
- Venue
- Timeline
- Seating Plan
- Gift Registry
- Documents
- Notes

Each module focuses on one area of planning while remaining connected through a shared workspace.

---

# Core Principles

The design of Tied Forever is guided by several principles.

## Simplicity

The interface should remain clean, intuitive and easy to navigate regardless of how much information has been added.

Users should spend their time planning their wedding rather than learning how to use the application.

---

## Flexibility

Tied Forever should adapt to different weddings rather than forcing every couple into the same planning process.

Where appropriate, users should be able to customise categories, organise information in ways that suit them and personalise their workspace.

---

## Collaboration

Wedding planning is rarely completed by one person.

The application should support collaboration while maintaining a clear understanding of who made changes and what information belongs to the shared wedding workspace.

---

## Single Source of Truth

Information should only need to be entered once.

Changes made in one area should be reflected wherever that information is used throughout the application.

---

## Scalability

Although the first release is intended for couples planning their own wedding, the architecture and product design should support future expansion without significant redesign.

---

# Target Audience

The primary audience is engaged couples planning their wedding together.

Secondary audiences may include:

- Family members assisting with planning
- Wedding coordinators
- Professional wedding planners
- Future commercial customers using Tied Forever as their primary planning platform

---

# Minimum Viable Product

Version 1 of Tied Forever includes the following modules:

- Dashboard
- Checklist
- Guests
- RSVP Management
- Budget
- Suppliers
- Venue
- Timeline
- Seating Plan
- Gift Registry
- Documents
- Notes
- Settings

The objective of the MVP is to provide a complete planning experience without relying on external planning tools.

---

# Out of Scope

The following features are intentionally excluded from the initial release:

- Online payments
- Supplier marketplace
- AI planning assistance
- Calendar integrations
- Email automation
- SMS messaging
- Native mobile applications

These features may be introduced in future releases once the core planning experience has been completed.

---

# Success Criteria

The first release of Tied Forever will be considered successful if a couple can plan and manage an entire wedding using the application without relying on external spreadsheets or planning software.

Success will be measured by:

- Ease of use
- Reliability
- Organisation of information
- Reduction in duplicated planning effort
- Clear visibility of planning progress
- Effective collaboration between members of the same wedding workspace

---

# Long-Term Vision

Tied Forever is intended to become more than a personal planning tool.

Future versions may introduce automation, integrations, guest-facing experiences and advanced collaboration features while maintaining the same guiding principle:

> Every wedding deserves one organised, reliable place where everything comes together.
